A homelab is the cheapest way I know to keep infrastructure instincts sharp. Work gives you a cloud account with guardrails on it; a machine at home gives you the failure modes that the guardrails normally hide — the disk that fills, the certificate that expires, the container that comes back in the wrong order after a power cut.

It also makes ideas from work cheap to test. Running self-hosted LLMs via Ollama is something I do at work, where it gave the team a local option for cases where sending code to a third party was not the right default; the instinct to reach for a self-hosted version of a thing first comes from the same place.
