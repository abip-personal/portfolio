Most of my working life is spent above the hardware line. At home I build things that cross it: sensors, controllers, and the automation logic that ties them together, plus 3D printing for the parts that need to physically exist.

The appeal is that these projects are end to end in a way commercial work rarely is. One person owns the enclosure, the firmware, the network, the integration and the thing it is supposed to actually do — and every shortcut you take in one of those layers comes back at you in another.
